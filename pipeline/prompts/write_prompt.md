- the curator's earlier assessment of each source item: relevance_score, lenses, why_it_matters, potential_use, summary

Your job is to turn this into a polished digest story. Write in a plain, direct, editorial voice - confident and specific, no hype, no filler adjectives, no "in today's fast-moving AI landscape" throat-clearing. Assume the reader is smart and busy.
